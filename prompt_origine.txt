USER STORY: 
The goal of Nook is to encourage the user to leave their screen and explore nature. 
This is the code  pipeline:
1. OBSERVE: A paccessible mobile-first interface (capture="environment") with dark mode posibility). 
The user snaps or uploads a photo from their phone.
2. ANALYZE: The Flask receives the image, validates the  type (jpeg, png, webp, gif), encodes it to base64 bytes, and directly queries the l Gemma 4 model using the google-genai client.
 The prompt sent to Gemma MUST demand a JSON object . The returned JSON must contain (written in a cautious, naturalist tone, max 25 words per string):
   - "seen": 3 to 6 direct visual observations such as (color, shape, texture).
   - "possible_identification": The possiblr identification might be still wrong, even if just rarely. It is better to state "possible", not confirming if not sure.
   - "alternatives": 1 or 2 other plausible options of why something had became what it was when taken photos
   - "hypotheses": 2 possible explanations regarding the state or origin of the found object
   - "uncertain": What the photo cannot tell due to its limitations. The AI Asks further questions in the investigation_question
   - "investigation_question": The AI asks further questions about the found objects for the AI to confirm hypothesis or suggestions, and helps people develop their observation and attention skills.
   - "observation_mission": An action to do outside immediately without touching or disturbing nature, like following ants' paths to their anthill, or give a rabit a leaf, or caressing barks to feel the relief, etc...
   - "atelier": A creative crafting task  that should be a concrete, safe action to do , based with the found objects (those can be a herbier with a clover, a drawing of a bark's patterns, a flower crown, a petal coloration, squirrel's best ohotoshoot, a christmas decoration made from pine cones, leaves, etc...).
3. NARRATE: A Flask endpoint POST /narrate receives the card text and uses the ElevenLabs TTS API (Model: `eleven_turbo_v2_5`, default Voice ID) to return a binar audio/mpeg stream. The JavaScript interface displays a "Field Card " button to fetch and play this audio stream directly in the browser.
4. INVESTIGATE The interface integrates the official ElevenLabs conversational voice widget (`<elevenlabs-convai>` loaded  via its script) by dynamically injecting the Agent ID. The user speaks to this voice guide in real-time to report their findings after returning from their outdoor mission.
5. PRESERVE: A field journal saves the session locally (base64 photo, Field Card JSON, atelier completion status, and user text notes) into the browser's localStorage. A history of past journal entries must be displayed upon loading the application, fot the hapiness to find memories back and remember what was learned through the little travel outside;.
